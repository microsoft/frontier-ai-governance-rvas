# Customer change and risk references

| Field | Customer-system reference |
|---|---|
| Change record | `__REQUIRED_CHANGE_REFERENCE__` |
| Policy owner | `__REQUIRED_POLICY_OWNER__` |
| Risk or exemption record | `__REQUIRED_RISK_REFERENCE_OR_NONE__` |

Azure Policy and Policy Insights hold the live definition, assignment, compliance, and exemption
state. Keep approval, risk acceptance, and review dates in the customer change or risk system. This
file stores pointers only.
