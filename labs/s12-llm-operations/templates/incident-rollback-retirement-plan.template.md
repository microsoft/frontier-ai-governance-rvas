# Incident, rollback, and retirement plan

Copy this blank, offline template into the customer's approved records system.
It records safe references to customer and supplier routes. It is not an
incident runbook, rollback instruction, production change approval, service
configuration, or retirement action. Do not enter live data, prompts, outputs,
credentials, configuration, or personal data.
