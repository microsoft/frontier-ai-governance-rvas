## Exception categories to consider

- Repeated owner, steward, or decision-authority gaps.
- Recurring evidence coverage, freshness, or attribution limitations.
- Shared platform, identity, data, evaluation, runtime, or cost dependency.
- Policy waiver, conflict, obsolete assumption, or unclear applicability.
- Finding that blocks more than one session outcome or portfolio decision.
