# S8 Runbook — Operating Review Definition

Use this runbook with the visible [S8 co-delivery activity](../../docs/s8-operate-measure/index.md).
The customer selects the review scope and evidence references; the facilitator
keeps the activity reference-only and records the decision boundary.

## Activity card

**90 minutes.** Entry condition: S6 closeout or deferral, an accountable
governance lead, a platform or service owner, and an approved records-system
location. Stop any question with no owner or authoritative evidence source.

1. Copy `templates/operating-review.template.md` and
   `templates/remediation-closure.template.md` into the approved customer
   records system.
2. Define one bounded population, review period, and decision. Record the
   evidence source and known coverage limitations for every chosen question.
3. Select only the relevant categories: control coverage, reliability,
   safety/risk, quality, cost, adoption, human review, business outcome, and
   remediation. Do not infer a metric or target from this template.
4. For each finding, record owner acceptance, target date, validation
   reference, recurrence check, exception/escalation path, and next review.
5. The governance lead approves, defers, or rejects the review definition.
   Retain the decision reference and limitations in the customer system.

No raw telemetry, identifiers, prompts, responses, costs, or business data
belong in this kit.
