# Facilitate the decision

| Phase | Time | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Set scope | 10 min | Confirm the pilot, records location, and decision owner. |
| Score evidence | 20 min | Separate supported scores from gaps and assumptions. |
| Select backlog | 15 min | Record one approve, defer, reject, or route result. |

Ask: **“Do we approve, defer, reject, or route the first governance backlog item?”**

Default to the customer's existing governance forum and backlog. Use another
route only when the sponsor records the reason, accountable owner, evidence
gap, acceptance criterion, and target date. Hand off the record to that owner;
do not represent the workshop as a system change or production approval.
