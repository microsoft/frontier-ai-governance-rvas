# AI Governance Platform

AI Governance Platform is a facilitator-led programme for customer decisions on
agent ownership, data, platform controls, assurance, and operation.

Start with [About AI Governance Platform](start/understand-rvas.md), then use
[Plan the engagement](start/plan-engagement.md) to prepare a bounded customer
decision before the [governance baseline and operating-model workshop](s0-foundations/index.md).

The work moves from accountability, through technical-path and assurance
decisions, to operating and portfolio improvement. Customers select only the
workshops needed for their question; they do not need to follow a fixed
sequence.

Use [Facilitate a co-delivery working session](delivery/facilitation-pattern.md)
for the 90-minute customer-led method. Use the
[Governance capability guide](reference/governance-capability-guide.md) for
capability context, the [Platform technical guide](reference/platform-technical-guide.md)
for platform evidence and ownership boundaries, and
[Quality, cost, latency, and rollout governance](reference/quality-cost-latency-guide.md)
for cross-workshop decision-record guidance.

Triage confirms that the use case is bounded enough to start, identifies obvious
safety or supplier-governance blockers, and routes work to customer records.
Decision authority, human approval, change approval, evidence retention,
incident routing, rollback, decommissioning, and legal or compliance conclusions
remain with the customer.
