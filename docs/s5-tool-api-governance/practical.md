# Practical activity: review one publication candidate

## What you will do

Customer platform and catalog owners review one real tool, API, or MCP service
candidate without publishing, connecting, or granting it access.

## Before you start

- Confirm the candidate source record, catalog owner, technical owner, and
  publication decision owner.
- Work from approved references only. Do not publish an entry, create an
  identity, or test a live connection.
- Stop if the caller identity, authority scope, or lifecycle owner is unknown.

## Customer-operated activity

1. Copy `labs/s5-tool-api-governance/templates/catalog-record.template.md` into
   the approved customer records system.
2. Record the purpose, accountable owner, caller identity, authority, version,
   placement, and lifecycle state.
3. Apply the publication criteria and record publish-ready, hold, reject,
   suspend, or withdraw with the required follow-up owner.

## What good looks like

A reviewer can explain who owns the candidate, how it is called, what authority
it exposes, and what must happen before publication.

## If the environment is not ready

Review a representative candidate from a customer-held record and state the
unknown source, identity, or authority detail.

## Keep and hand over

Keep the record and decision in approved customer records. Hand catalog, API
gateway, identity, lifecycle, and release work to the customer owners.
