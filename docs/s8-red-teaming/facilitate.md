# Facilitate the decision

| Phase | Time | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Confirm safety | 20 min | Reconfirm authorization, SOC window, and stop conditions. |
| Review | 30 min | Keep findings tied to the authorized scope. |
| Decide | 40 min | Choose remediation, accepted risk, blocked, or re-test. |

Ask: **“Who can stop the run now?”**
