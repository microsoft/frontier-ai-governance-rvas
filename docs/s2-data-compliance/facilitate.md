# Facilitate the decision

| Phase | Time | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Map path | 10 min | Confirm scope, data owner, and investigation route. |
| Review evidence | 25 min | Separate supported Purview coverage from gaps. |
| Decide and hand off | 10 min | Record approve, defer, reject, or route. |

Ask: **“Do we approve, defer, reject, or route the proposed data-use enforcement
decision?”**

Default to supported Microsoft Purview controls and customer change review.
An exception requires documented workload/support limits, owner, evidence
reference, acceptance criterion, and target date. Route S3 platform questions,
S6 runtime security, S7 evaluation, and S11 operations to their owners. Do not
deploy enforcement or grant production approval.
