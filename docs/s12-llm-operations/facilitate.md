# Facilitate the decision

| Phase | Time | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Set scope | 10 min | Confirm customer control, asset boundary, owners, and exclusions. |
| Review | 30 min | Separate evidence, gaps, and dependencies from assumptions. |
| Decide | 50 min | Approve, defer, or reject the operating model and assign backlog handoffs. |

Ask: **"What can the customer materially change, and who owns the route when it changes?"**
