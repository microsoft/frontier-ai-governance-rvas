# Practical activity: answer one operating question

## What you will do

Customer owners run one operating-review question for a defined population and
period using approved evidence references.

## Before you start

- Confirm the population, period, decision owner, evidence owner, and approved
  records location.
- Use references and safe summaries only. Do not copy telemetry, prompts,
  responses, identifiers, or cost records into this repository.
- Stop if the evidence cannot support the selected question.

## Customer-operated activity

1. Copy `labs/s11-operate-measure/templates/operating-review.template.md` to
   the customer records system.
2. Select one question, such as control coverage, a reliability change, an
   open risk, or cost ownership.
3. Record the evidence reference, coverage limit, interpretation owner, finding
   or no-finding, and escalation or remediation route.

## What good looks like

The customer can answer one bounded operating question and explain what the
evidence does not cover. A trend or alert is an input to a decision, not closure.

## If the environment is not ready

Define the question and record the exact evidence, owner, and collection cadence
needed to answer it at the next review.

## Keep and hand over

Keep the review reference and decision in customer records. Hand alerts,
remediation checks, exceptions, and cost actions to their named owners.
