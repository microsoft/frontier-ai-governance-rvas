# Practical activity: prioritize one portfolio action

## What you will do

Customer portfolio leaders review one bounded portfolio slice and select one
evidence-backed governance priority.

## Before you start

- Confirm the population, review period, portfolio owner, risk owner, and
  approved records location.
- Use approved references only. Do not aggregate raw evidence or create a
  portfolio score in this repository.
- Stop if the scope, decision owner, or coverage limit is unknown.

## Customer-operated activity

1. Copy `labs/s12-portfolio-governance/templates/portfolio-review.template.md`
   into the approved customer records system.
2. Add references for a small set of in-scope exceptions, dependencies, or
   maturity questions, including coverage and freshness limits.
3. Compare the candidate actions, select one priority, and name the owner,
   decision route, and next S0 question to revisit.

## What good looks like

The selected priority has a stated risk, dependency, evidence basis, owner, and
review date. A gap in coverage remains visible rather than becoming a portfolio
claim.

## If the environment is not ready

Use the portfolio template with approved references and record the missing
population, evidence, or authority prerequisite.

## Keep and hand over

Keep the portfolio decision and roadmap in customer records. Hand approved
investment, policy, exception, and funding work to the relevant customer
process, then schedule the next baseline review.
