# S12 · Portfolio Governance & Continuous Improvement

**Facilitator deck**

Governance lead · Portfolio owner · Risk owner · 90-minute report-only portfolio review

Note:
Welcome and set the report-only boundary. This session works from approved references only. It does not import raw records, query live systems, create a dashboard, make production or policy changes, approve funding, or issue a compliance conclusion. Roles: facilitator, governance lead, portfolio owner, risk owner, evidence owner, and specialists only for decisions they own.

---

## One portfolio decision

> **"What roadmap decision can this portfolio review support — and what remains unknown?"**

By the end, the customer has a portfolio review, roadmap priorities, and S0 questions to revisit.

Note:
Keep the session tied to a bounded portfolio question, population, period, and decision owner. The review should make patterns visible without pretending aggregation is proof.

---

## Why this matters

- Local reviews can find actions and still miss portfolio patterns.
- S12 looks across agents for recurring exceptions and shared dependencies.
- It surfaces investment trade-offs, maturity movement, and policy questions.
- Aggregation is not proof.

Note:
Frame S12 as the connective tissue across sessions. It helps leaders make the next roadmap decision, but it keeps scope, coverage, ownership, and uncertainty visible.

---

## A portfolio is a decision view

- Portfolio governance connects decisions across agents and scopes.
- It uses references, scope statements, and limits.
- Leaders can see dependencies without copying operational records into one place.
- Population, period, and evidence coverage must stay visible.

Note:
Do not let the group turn this into a data-lake or dashboard exercise. The portfolio view is a decision view built from approved references and explicit limits.

---

## Evidence keeps its limits when aggregated

- A reference supports only the observation and scope it describes.
- Freshness, exclusions, collection limits, and interpretation owner stay with it.
- Combining partial references does not prove complete coverage.
- Unknown impact, ownership, or scope remains unresolved.

Note:
This is the core discipline of the session. Aggregation can show patterns, but it does not erase limits. If a source is partial, the portfolio conclusion remains partial.

---

## Exceptions expose concentrations

- Repeated ownership gaps
- Recurring evidence coverage limits
- Shared platform or identity dependencies
- Repeated policy waivers or unresolved high-impact findings
- Risks that block more than one session outcome

Note:
An exception is not automatically a portfolio risk. It matters when its effect, recurrence, shared dependency, or decision consequence is clear. Unknown impact stays unknown and needs review or escalation.

---

## Investment priority is a transparent trade-off

State criteria:

- risk, dependency, and evidence strength
- expected governance value
- effort and timing
- accountable owner and decision status
- for model capability: evaluation, cost, data governance, version owner, and assurance dependency

Note:
Ranking informs authorized decision-makers. It is not a promise of benefit, a spending approval, or a change authorization. Without customer-held references, model capability investments, including fine-tuning, remain proposals with explicit gaps; S12 does not approve training, deployment, or funding.

---

## Maturity and policy movement need evidence

- Compare against the same question, scope, and scale — or clearly state the change.
- Evidence can support movement, no movement, or unresolved assessment.
- Policy questions need proposal, reason, affected scope, owner, and review route.
- Activity completion does not prove new capability.

Note:
Separate maturity movement from policy evolution. Both need evidence and a decision route. Neither is approved merely because the portfolio review identified it.

## The activity — how we'll work

- **Timebox:** 90 minutes · **six activities**
- **To start:** records location, decision owners, S0 baseline references, session outcomes, review period, and population.
- Missing evidence is a visible limit.
- Do not replace missing evidence with a template, estimate, or aggregate label.

Note:
Review the portfolio-reporting, prioritization/reinvestment, and continuous-improvement technical decision menus first. If the scope, records location, trusted references, or decision owner are missing, block the dependent item.

---

## Step 1 — Set the portfolio question · 10 min

Define the population, period, decision, records location, and stop condition.

> **"What portfolio decision can this review support?"**

A broad ambition without a decision owner is blocked.

Note:
Start with the decision. If the review cannot name what leaders will decide and who owns that decision, record the blocker instead of widening the scope.

---

## Step 2 — Aggregate evidence references · 15 min

Enter only references, scope, freshness, coverage, and limits in the portfolio review template.

> **"What does this reference cover, and what does it exclude?"**

A reference does not make uncovered scopes reliable.

Note:
Keep raw records out. The customer retains approved references and coverage limits in their records system. The portfolio view preserves limits rather than smoothing them away.

---

## Step 3 — Review exceptions and portfolio risk · 20 min

Record recurring, cross-scope, or decision-blocking exceptions with owners, dependencies, and escalation routes.

> **"Which exception can affect more than one scope?"**

Unknown impact stays unknown.

Note:
Do not turn unknown impact into low risk. Record the owner, dependency, disposition, due date, and escalation or review route for material items.

---

## Step 4 — Interpret maturity movement and policy questions · 15 min

Compare observations with the relevant S0 baseline by domain.

> **"What changed in observed practice?"**
> **"What policy decision is being requested?"**

Proposed movement or policy change is not approval.

Note:
Capture supporting references for movement, no movement, or unresolved status. Policy questions become proposals with an owner and review path; they do not change policy in the room.

---

## Step 5 — Prioritize investment · 15 min

Rank a small set of candidate investments using stated criteria.

> **"What would this unlock or reduce?"**

Ranking informs a decision. It is not funding approval or a benefit claim.

Note:
Use risk, dependency, evidence strength, expected governance value, effort, timing, and owner readiness. Keep the list small enough to support an actual roadmap decision.

---

## Step 6 — Decide the roadmap and feed S0 · 15 min

Approve, defer, or reject priorities. Assign owners and dates.

> **"What will S0 reassess, when, and with what evidence?"**

Record remaining risk and unknowns alongside the roadmap.

Note:
S12 does not overwrite S0. It supplies a dated roadmap and a short list of baseline domains or questions that need reassessment. The next S0 review remains evidence-based and customer-owned.

---

## Verification & evidence

- [ ] Portfolio question, population, review period, owners, and records location are explicit.
- [ ] Each aggregated item has reference, scope, freshness, coverage limit, and interpretation owner.
- [ ] Each material exception or portfolio risk has disposition, owner, due date, and escalation or review route.
- [ ] Each maturity movement, policy question, and investment priority identifies support and decision status.
- [ ] The roadmap names the next governance review and S0 questions or domains to revisit.

Note:
Retain completed artifacts only in the approved records system. In any delivery workspace, retain references and classification or retention metadata only. Do not retain raw evidence, names, operational data, or meeting notes.

---

## Change boundary & hand-off

- S12 is report-only.
- It makes no dashboard, live-data, production, access, configuration, policy, funding, or compliance-certification decision.
- Approved changes use separate authority, change, assurance, and records processes.
- The portfolio roadmap feeds the next S0 baseline review.

Note:
Close with the decision guardrail: aggregation reveals relationships and limits; it never proves control effectiveness, complete coverage, or compliance. Exceptions and policy proposals stay in accountable governance or change paths until separately decided.
