# Practical activity: admit one real candidate

## What you will do

Customer engineering and governance owners create an admission record for one
genuine agent candidate and decide what it must satisfy before the next stage.

## Before you start

- Confirm a bounded candidate, engineering owner, service owner, and decision
  owner.
- Use customer-approved architecture and delivery references. Do not copy source
  code, credentials, or configuration into this repository.
- Stop if the candidate's purpose or authority boundary is not understood.

## Customer-operated activity

1. Copy `labs/s4-agent-engineering/templates/admission-record.template.md` to
   the customer records system.
2. Record the candidate's purpose, authority, selected implementation path,
   evidence gaps, and admission requirements.
3. Select one requirement that must be owned before admission, such as an
   identity, data, evaluation, or runtime-assurance dependency.

## What good looks like

The customer has a specific candidate, a bounded authority statement, and a
clear admission, defer, reject, or evidence-gap decision.

## If the environment is not ready

Use a sanitized representative candidate and state the assumptions. Do not
claim that its result admits a customer workload.

## Keep and hand over

Keep the admission record in approved customer records. Hand selected
implementation and release requirements to the customer's engineering,
architecture, security, and change processes.
