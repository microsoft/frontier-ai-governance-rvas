Chapter 1 of 6

Module scope#

What we will do#

Objective. Turn "which models may we use" from a conversation into a record and a policy that reads from it.

Fill in a model approval register: for each model, its publisher, asset ID, source category, hosting route, approved deployment types, processing location, approvers, and review date. Then assign the two built-in Foundry model-deployment policies at the approved resource group, taking their parameters straight from that register. The check reads both live assignments and confirms their effect and allowed values still match.

Why it matters#

Problem. A model deployment approval needs an inspectable record of who approved the model, why, and which deployment routes are allowed. When that record lives in a spreadsheet or a conversation, Azure Policy cannot apply it and a developer can deploy a model no reviewer saw.

Solution. One register holds the decision, and Azure Policy evaluates the same values at deployment time.

Boundaries#

This module sits outside the numbered sequence and follows Sessions 01 and 03.

It changes two policy assignments at one resource group and adds the approval register. Azure Policy holds the live allowed values; the register holds the reasoning behind them and is the file the parameter file reads.

The policies evaluate model deployments in Microsoft Foundry. They do not govern what a model is used for after deployment, and they do not replace the Session 09 evaluation gates or the Session 03 version-pinning and quota decisions. Deployment types stay with Session 03; this module records which types each approved model may use and leaves their enforcement there.

Module preparation

Who should join

  • AI platform and cloud governance engineers
  • Security, privacy, and legal reviewers who approve model use
  • Azure Policy owners

What you need

  • Session 01 has deployed the Microsoft Foundry baseline in the approved nonproduction resource group.
  • Session 03 has recorded the workload's processing-location requirement and the deployment types allowed for it.
  • The policy owner holds Owner or Resource Policy Contributor at the exact resource group where the assignments are created.
  • The model reviewers can read a model card in the Foundry model catalog and copy its publisher name and model asset ID.
  • The approved change system holds any earlier model-deployment policy assignment that this module replaces.
