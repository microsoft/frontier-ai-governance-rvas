# Repository instructions

## Writing

- Invoke `/humanize-writing` before creating or editing prose.
- Use the `clear-thinker` voice unless the user names another voice.
- Keep language understandable, simplify complexity while still sounding technical and professional, do not make convoluted/foggy phrases, use weird wording and phrases, keep the language honest and human.
- Do not overcomplicate concepts, text, code just for the sake of being extra-complete: write what is needed without exceeding. Do not add things that have a diminishing return. When in doubt, spin a subagent that will do solely the judgement on completeness and follow what it says.
- Avoid hyper-puntualization of concepts and un-necessary complexity: the best minds express complex concepts clearly.
- Don't build "castles in the air".
- Apply this rule to public site copy, documentation, labels, headings, workshop material, and facilitator notes.
- Preserve technical facts, Microsoft product names, dates, citations, and governance terminology exactly.
- Finish every prose change with a pass against the skill's AI-pattern dictionary. Remove canned transitions, inflated claims, repetitive sentence shapes, and banned AI vocabulary.
- If a sentence still sounds generated after that pass, rewrite it before shipping.

## Visual identity

- Treat `PRODUCT.md` and each session's `session.yaml` as the content authority. Never inherit curriculum copy, claims, or session numbering from a visual reference.
- Reuse the supplied RVAP logos and official Microsoft product icons. Do not redraw, recolor, crop, rotate, or distort them.
